# Changelog

## 1.1.0

Six colours and three backgrounds weren't really enough to make the app feel like yours, so this update is mostly about customisation. I also added Discord status so friends can see what you're using and grab it themselves.

- Colour picker: choose any accent colour you want, as well as the six presets. Your custom colour is remembered if you switch back to a preset
- The anger mark and mouth on the app icon now change to match your accent colour, in the sidebar, on the overlay and in the taskbar
- Five new backgrounds: Graphite, Forest, Plum, Mocha, and Match accent, which uses a dark shade of your accent colour
- Backgrounds are picked from small previews instead of text buttons
- Discord status shows "Checking MM2 trades" and how many trades you've checked this session, with buttons to download the app and join the Discord. It can be turned off in Settings > General
- The bottom of Settings now says where the official download is, with links to the GitHub and the Discord, so people can tell the real app apart from reuploads

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Hotkey scan of both trade offers with an in-game overlay showing totals, demand and a verdict
- Reads item names with the text recognition built into Windows, no extra installs
- Handles wrapped names, quantity badges, Chroma tags and items that share a name
- Tier values (L, R, U, C) converted with the same ladder as the MM2Values calculator
- Demand, rarity and stability ratings from MM2Values
- Value browser with search, rarity filters and sorting
- Themes, overlay size and opacity, compact mode, adjustable fair-trade range and sound volume
